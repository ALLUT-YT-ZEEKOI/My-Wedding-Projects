LuxeHalls Project Features Summary

Project Overview: A premium, multi-tenant marketplace platform for luxury event spaces and banquet halls, featuring a highly aesthetic, modern UI.

1. Customer Features (Frontend)

  • Aesthetic UI/UX: Completely redesigned homepage with a minimalist, luxury aesthetic, glassmorphic elements, and editorial typography.
  • Advanced Search: Floating search bar allowing customers to search seamlessly by exact Location and Date.
  • Venue Collection: Replaced standard lists/maps with a premium Magazine-style Grid layout for browsing venues.
  • Favorites & Wishlist: Customers can favourite halls and save them to their wishlist for later review.
  • Detailed Profiles: Customers can view comprehensive hall profiles including pricing, policies, capacity breakdown, and photo galleries.

2. Vendor Features (Vendor Panel)

  • Premium Onboarding: Split-screen luxury UI for Vendor Login and Registration.
  • Approval Workflow: New vendors are placed in a "Pending Approval" state until verified by the Super Admin.
  • Venue Management: Vendors can add, edit, and preview their halls.
  • Accurate Search Indexing: When adding a hall, vendors specify a strict "Searchable Location / City" ensuring their venue accurately appears in customer searches.
  • Granular Controls: Vendors manage seating/floating capacities, morning/evening/full-day slot pricing, and strict venue policies (catering, music, etc.).
  • Subscription Tiers: Vendors choose from tiered subscription plans (e.g., Free, Premium) that limit the number of halls they can list.
  • Dashboard & Analytics: View upcoming bookings, revenue, and customer inquiries.

3. Super Admin Features (Admin Panel)

  • Vendor Approvals: Dedicated queue to review and approve newly registered vendor accounts.
  • Platform Analytics: Real-time dashboard tracking Total Revenue, Active Vendors, Total Halls, and Monthly Bookings.
  • Master Data Management: Full control over global variables such as Amenities, Event Types, and Subscription Plan limits/pricing.
  • User Management: Oversee all customers, vendors, and internal staff roles.
  • Comprehensive Reports: Generate and view financial and operational reports across the entire platform.
